About Our Repossession Auto Financing

A repossession follows you around on paper, but it doesn't have to follow you into your next car. Our repossession auto financing program was built for drivers in exactly this position — people who need reliable transportation now and a lender willing to look past what happened before.

Instead of treating a repossession as a dead end, we treat it as one data point next to your current income, your job stability, and how much you can put toward a payment today. That's what lets us approve applicants that traditional banks and larger dealer groups automatically decline.

  • Approval decisions based on current income, not past repossession history
  • In-house financing, so there's no waiting on outside bank approval
  • Flexible payment schedules built around your pay cycle
  • A path to rebuild credit with consistent, on-time payments

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from bad credit, bankruptcy, and repossession applicants.

Can I get approved for a car loan after a repossession?

Yes. Our repossession auto financing program is built specifically for drivers who've had a vehicle repossessed. We look at your current income and ability to pay today, not just what happened in the past.

Do you check my credit score?

We offer no credit check options through our in-house financing program. For some offers we do a soft check, but a low or damaged score never disqualifies you here.

I filed for bankruptcy. Can I still qualify?

Absolutely. Our bankruptcy car loan program is designed for exactly this situation, whether your case is active, discharged, or recently closed.

Do I need a down payment?

Not always. We offer no down payment options for qualified buyers, and flexible plans for everyone else based on your budget.

What documents do I need to apply?

Bring a valid ID, proof of income, and proof of residence. Our team will walk you through anything else needed for your specific approval.
